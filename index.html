Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common inquiries about our classes, schedules, and systems

How can I register and start my classes? +

Registration is fast and seamless. You can click on any "Enroll Now" button to message us directly on WhatsApp, or simply fill out the contact form below. Our dedicated coordination support desk will instantly contact you to understand your specific proficiency level, discuss goals, and book your first interactive trial session at a time that works best for you.

Do you offer free evaluation trial classes? +

Yes, absolutely. We offer premium, comprehensive evaluation trial sessions completely free of charge. This allows students and parents to interact with our instructors, evaluate our custom digital learning portal tools, and experience our structured framework firsthand with absolutely no financial risk or initial subscription commitments.

What is the standard duration of each class? +

Each personalized one-on-one private session is structured for 30 minutes. Modern pedagogical research proves that focused, high-engagement 30-minute blocks maximize retention and prevent digital fatigue, making it highly effective for both young learners and busy professionals who want to maintain steady linguistic progress.

Can I adjust or reschedule my class timings later? +

Yes, our system is designed to accommodate dynamic global schedules. Since we serve students across multiple international timezones (including the UK, Europe, and the Middle East), you can smoothly coordinate temporary or permanent schedule shifts directly with our administrative support desk to ensure your education is never interrupted.

Is this academy suitable for absolute beginners? +

Completely. Our foundational programs, such as Noorani Qaida and Elementary Arabic, are crafted specifically for absolute beginners. We break down complex phonetic articulation points and basic word structures into highly manageable steps, giving you a strong, verified foundational root before moving on to advanced texts.
