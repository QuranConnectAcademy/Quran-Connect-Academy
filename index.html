Sheikh Muhammad Abdullah

Quran Connect Academy operates under the direct guidance of Sheikh Muhammad Abdullah, a professional educator. He holds a formal Master's degree in Arabic & Islamic Sciences along with a specialized higher specialization focusing on classical Arabic grammar and textual syntax from a recognized traditional institution.

With more than 7+ years of international teaching experience, Sheikh Muhammad Abdullah has designed clear, systematic pathways to help global students read and understand text smoothly. His classes guide students to recite the Holy Quran using the precise, beautiful phonetic rules matching the verified traditional frameworks of the Imams of the Haram.

Alongside his deep classical Islamic education, Sheikh Muhammad Abdullah is an LLB Graduate (Law). This background brings an exceptionally high standard of logical thinking, structural clarity, and modern professional organization into traditional classical learning models. By combining deep traditional roots with formal legal reasoning, he ensures that students receive highly clear, accurate, and professional instruction.
